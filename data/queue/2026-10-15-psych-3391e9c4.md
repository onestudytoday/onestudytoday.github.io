## Slide 1 - What it means for you

**Title:** If you or someone you know has restless legs syndrome, the first pill your doctor reaches for should have changed

**Basis:** stated    <!-- stated = the paper says it; inferred = our read, must stay conditional -->

Alpha-2-delta drugs are now preferred first-line therapy, displacing dopamine agonists. The shift follows evidence that dopamine agonists carry big long-term risk of worsening symptoms.

The 2025 sleep medicine guideline formalizes the change. Treatment now starts with finding triggers, fixing iron, then adding tailored medicine.

## Slide 2 - The result

**Title:** Restless legs syndrome affects seven percent of adults worldwide and substantially impacts sleep and quality of life

The review consolidates recent evidence on diagnosis and care. The key shift: dopamine agonists are out as preferred chronic therapy. Alpha-2-delta drugs now lead.

A stepwise approach starting with triggers and iron status offers the best outcomes across the disease spectrum.

## Caveats

- This is a clinical review summarizing existing evidence and guidelines, not a new experimental study with original data.
- The abstract does not detail how much alpha-2-delta drugs reduce symptoms or how often worsening occurs with dopamine agonists.
