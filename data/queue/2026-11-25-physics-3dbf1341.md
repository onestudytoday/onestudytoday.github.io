**Headline:** **A fact costs work to create**, and past a critical angle no energy can make it objective.

## Slide 1 - What it could mean

**Title:** Recording an event so it becomes objective fact has a thermodynamic price that scales with redundancy.

**Basis:** stated    <!-- stated = the paper says it; inferred = our read, must stay conditional -->

The team computed the minimum work to create records at different levels of redundancy. Beyond a critical misalignment angle, they report objectivity becomes unreachable at any energy cost.

This links the emergence of classical facts to energy and entropy, not just information.

## Slide 2 - Results

**Title:** Work required to create one event record scaled linearly with redundancy at the Landauer coefficient.

The model used a central qubit writing to N thermal registers. Work needed was at least R times Boltzmann constant times temperature times a function of error rate.

Erasing accessed records yielded net work gain beyond the first copy.

## Slide 3 - What they did

**Title:** The team modeled a driven qubit broadcasting records into registers with work sources and thermal baths.

They calculated exact thermodynamic costs for creating, keeping, and destroying records in this minimal broadcast model. They derived closed-form expressions for optimal work in the pointer basis.

They also computed power needed to maintain a record as a nonequilibrium steady state.

## Caveats

- This is a preprint. It has not been peer reviewed yet, so no independent expert has checked the work.
- This is a theoretical model with idealized conditions. Real physical systems may behave differently from the minimal broadcast setup used here.
