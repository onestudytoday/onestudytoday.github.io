That could guide which quantum systems are useful for tasks that need certainty in one test to guarantee ignorance in another.

Simpler dimensions were already solved. Dimension six was the simplest case still open.

## Slide 2 - What they found

**Title:** The team reports every triple of these bases belongs to one family or is one isolated triple.

Since none extend to a fourth basis, this also shows no four such bases exist in dimension six.

These bases describe tests where certainty in one gives no information about the others.

## Slide 3 - The mechanism

**Title:** The proof rests on showing almost all of these matrices form a connected space in the right topology.

Connectedness reduced the problem to finitely many overlapping balls in four dimensions.

The team used interval math to certify the balls cover the space, then found all third bases for every pair in each ball.
