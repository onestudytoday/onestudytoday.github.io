## Slide 2 - The result

**Title:** Light-based mapping found distinct patterns that reliably told bipolar disorder from other conditions.

Researchers collected hair from people with bipolar disorder, repeated depression, mixed anxiety and depression, and healthy controls. They recorded 30 readings from each 0.5 cm segment.

The bipolar samples grouped apart. The chemical signature was clear enough to tell the conditions apart.

## Slide 3 - How it works

**Title:** The method uses infrared light to read chemical signals in hair cortex without damaging the sample.

Each reading captures a chemical pattern. The data were smoothed and then analyzed to reveal patterns tied to different mood disorders.

The technique measures how molecules in the hair vibrate when hit with infrared light.

## Caveats
